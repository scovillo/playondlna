New in version 1.15:

- More reliable streaming with multiple network connections: PlayOnDlna now targets your Wi-Fi network
- Playlists now show whether playback is managed by PlayOnDlna or your player
- If your player struggles with playlists, you can permanently let PlayOnDlna manage them
- Favorites are clearer to manage and load faster
- Shared links are accepted reliably from more apps
- Players are discovered more reliably
